3	How do I order?
4	When will my order be ready?
5	Standard print jobs are made 1–2 business days after we approve your file and receive your payment. For large quantities, installation or special jobs, the time is written in your quote.
6	Is the price in the calculator final?
7	How can I pay?
8	You can pay by card (through PayTR) or by bank transfer. For print-ready orders you pay on the website when you order. Because every job is made just for you, there is no cash on delivery.
9	Which file formats do you accept?
10	We prefer PDF, AI, EPS and SVG. Text should be turned into outlines and the cut line should be on its own layer. PNG, JPG and TIFF files should be at least 150 dpi at real size. When you upload your file on the product page, we check this automatically.
11	How long do stickers and prints last outdoors?
12	Stickers and cut vinyl printed on outdoor vinyl keep their colour for many years. We print with ink made to resist sun, rain and outdoor weather.
